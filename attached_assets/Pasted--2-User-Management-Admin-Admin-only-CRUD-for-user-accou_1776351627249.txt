### 2. User Management (Admin)
- Admin-only CRUD for user accounts
- Role assignment (`user` / `admin`)
- One-time admin bootstrap via `/api/setup`

### 3. Client Management
- Full CRUD operations with pagination and sorting
- Auto-generated client codes (e.g., "AC001")
- Search and filter (active/inactive)
- ISO certifications tracking
- CSV import/export
- Bulk delete and bulk mark-inactive

### 4. Consultant Management
- CRUD with auto-seeded defaults (TY, PL, PW)
- Short code assignment
- Assignment to invoices for revenue tracking

### 5. Invoice System
- **Status workflow** (session API): Draft → Pending approval → Approved → Sent → Revised → Paid (there is no separate “viewed” step in the status model)
- Line items with quantity, price, tax rate
- Discount support (percentage or fixed)
- Multi-currency (HKD, USD, CNY, MOP)
- Consultant assignment (up to 3)
- PDF generation with company branding
- Auto-generated invoice numbers (YYMM-NNN)
- Email invoice PDF to client (SMTP or Gmail API)
- Send payment reminder emails
- Bulk delete and bulk status updates
- CSV import/export

### 6. Quotation System
- **Status workflow** (session API): Draft → Pending approval → Sent → Approved → Confirmed or Canceled (no “viewed” step)
- Convert to Invoice, convert to Job Order only, or **Confirm-and-Convert** (creates both Invoice and Job Order in one step)
- Convert to Job Order
- Lookup by quotation number
- Line items management with remarks and terms
- PDF generation and email delivery
- Reusable quotation templates
- Auto-generated quotation numbers (QYYMM-NNN)

### 7. Job Orders
- Manual creation and edit
- Convert from Quotation or from Invoice
- **AI PDF Extraction** using DeepSeek API
- Extract company info and line items from uploaded PDFs
- PDF generation and email delivery
- Auto-generated job order numbers (JOYYMM-NNN)

### 8. Payment Tracking
- Record payments against invoices
- Multiple payment methods (bank transfer, cash, check, credit card, other)
- Auto-update invoice paid status
- Payment reversal (delete) with automatic status rollback
- Receipt file upload per payment

### 9. Recurring Invoices
- Schedule invoices to recur on a defined frequency
- Enable/disable individual schedules
- Batch-process all due recurring invoices via single endpoint

### 10. Revenue Dashboard
- Summary stats card (revenue, invoices, quotations, payments)
- Monthly revenue bar chart
- Revenue by consultant breakdown
- Year selector
